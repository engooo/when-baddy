# Product

## Register

Product (app / tool). Design serves the function of finding available badminton/pickleball courts.

## Users & Purpose

**Users:** Sydney-based badminton and pickleball players, from casual to regular. They visit to check court availability for today or upcoming days before booking.

**Context:** Mobile-first for convenience (checking on-the-go). Desktop for detailed planning and comparison.

**Primary job:** Find available court slots at specific venues and times, compare prices, and jump to book.

**Workflow:** Land → Pick date / filter → Scan table for open slots → Click to book.

## Brand Personality

**Personality:** Straightforward, efficient, utilitarian. No decoration; dense information.

**Anti-references:** Overly corporate SaaS, glossy gradients, heavy ornamentation, slow animations.

**Principles:**
- Content is the hero; UI gets out of the way.
- Mobile scannability is non-negotiable.
- Every pixel earns its place.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 contrast on text).
- Reduced motion respected on all interactions.
- Keyboard accessible; form and table navigation clear.

## Current State

Early-stage MVP with working scraping, aggregation, and booking flow. Recently simplified header and refined toggle/filter UI for clarity.
